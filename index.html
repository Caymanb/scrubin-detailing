Local conditions

Why paint doesn't last here

A detail in Southwest Riverside County is not the same job as a detail on the coast. Inland heat, mineral-heavy water and windblown dust are unusually hard on paint, trim and interiors — which is why protection matters more here.

Hard water & sprinkler spotting
Inland water is heavily mineralized. Sprinkler overspray dries fast in the heat and etches minerals straight into clearcoat. Clay bar decontamination and a ceramic coating are the real fix.
Santa Ana winds & dust
Fall through spring, northeast winds gust hard enough to trigger windblown dust advisories across Temecula, Murrieta and Lake Elsinore. That fine grit sandblasts paint and loads up your cabin filter.
Relentless UV and inland heat
Summers here bake dashboards, crack leather, fade trim and yellow headlights. Paint sealants, trim coatings and interior UV protection buy years of life back.
Spring pollen & wine-country road dust
Pollen is abrasive and turns acidic when it gets damp. Add the unpaved shoulders around Wine Country, De Luz and the still-building French Valley corridor and weekly washing genuinely pays off.

Straight answers

Mobile detailing FAQs

Do you really come to me — and is there a trip fee?

Yes. Scrubin Detailing is fully mobile. We arrive with our own water and power and work wherever your vehicle is parked — home, apartment complex, office lot or storage yard. There is no trip fee, travel fee or convenience fee anywhere in Temecula, Murrieta, Menifee, French Valley, Winchester or Wildomar.

How much does mobile detailing cost in Temecula?

Our full inside-and-out details start at $115 for a sedan (Basic Detail) and run to $400 for an oversized vehicle on the 1 Step Paint Correction Detail. Interior-only details start at $75, exterior washes at $60, and the Ceramic Coating Package runs $550–$750 depending on vehicle size. RVs and boats are priced per foot ($7–$15/ft). Every price is published on this page — scroll up to Pricing.

How long does a detail take?

A Basic Detail is typically 1.5–2 hours. A Full Detail usually runs 2.5–4 hours. The 1 Step Paint Correction Detail and the Ceramic Coating Package are longer appointments — plan on most of the day. RVs and boats vary with length. We'll give you a realistic window when we confirm your booking.

What's the difference between wax, sealant and ceramic coating?

Wax is the shortest-lived — our 6-month paint protection wax (included in the Full Detail and the Exterior Clay & Wax package) adds gloss and real protection. A ceramic coating goes much further: a semi-permanent barrier that bonds to the clearcoat, lasts four years, and is the only one that really stands up to Temecula Valley conditions — hard-water sprinkler spotting, relentless UV and windblown dust. Coated paint is also noticeably slicker, so future washes take less time and less contact.

Can you get hard water spots off my paint?

Usually, yes. Fresh water spots come off with a clay bar decontamination. Spots that have been baking in the sun for months are etched into the clearcoat and need machine polishing — that's our 1 Step Paint Correction Detail or the Ceramic Coating Package. Text us a photo in good light and we'll tell you honestly which one your paint needs.

Do you remove pet hair, stains and odors?

Yes. Our Premium Interior Detail ($130–$170) includes seat and carpet extraction on cloth seats, carpet shampooing, steam cleaning, and scrub and decontamination on all plastics and leather. Heavy pet hair is billed as an add-on at $30 per hour because it genuinely takes extra time. We'll always confirm that with you before we start, never after.

Do you detail RVs, boats, jet skis and dirt bikes?

All of them. RV and trailer exterior washes are $7 per foot, boat exterior washes $7 per foot, boat full details $15 per foot, jet skis $150 each (trailer included), and motorcycles or dirt bikes $50. If you're launching at Lake Skinner or Diamond Valley Lake, book the day before — both are MWD reservoirs with pre-launch inspection requirements, and a clean vessel makes that a lot smoother.

What do you need from me on the day?

A parking spot with a little room to work around the vehicle, and keys or access to the interior. We bring our own water, power and everything else. If you're in an apartment or a gated community, just let us know where to park and how to get in.

How often should I get my car detailed here?

For most daily drivers in the valley, a full detail every 3–4 months with a maintenance detail in between keeps paint protected and interiors from ever getting bad. Returning clients get our Maintenance Detail at $85–$100 within 60 days of a full service. Given how fast pollen, dust and sprinkler water build up here, staying on a schedule is cheaper than fixing neglect later.
